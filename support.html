How do I log a set?

Tap an exercise on Today. Type the kg and reps and tap ✓. Leave a box empty and ✓ uses the grey numbers from your last set.

How do I add or remove sets, swap or pair exercises?

Open the exercise and tap ✎ Edit. Change the number of sets, swap it, make a superset or delete it.

How do I change split or build my own?

Tap ⇄ Change split on Today. Tap any split to look at it first. + Build your own lets you name it, set the days and pick the exercises.

My workouts aren't showing on my other device

Log in with the same email on both devices (Profile → Log in). Pull down fresh data with PULL on the sync bar at the top of Today.

I forgot my password

On the log-in screen, type your email and tap Forgot password?. You'll get a reset link.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Delete account. Your login and every workout stored in the cloud are erased. Read the privacy policy.
