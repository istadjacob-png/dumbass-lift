## Description
DUMBASS LIFT is the lift tracker for people who just want to lift.

Open it, see today's workout, log your sets. That's it.

LOG EVERY SET
• One row per set: kg, reps, ✓
• Leave it empty and ✓ repeats your last numbers
• "Last: 100kg × 8 reps" under every exercise, so you know what to beat

SEE EVERYTHING YOU'VE DONE
• Every workout, every exercise, every set and rep, saved
• Progress charts for every lift
• Weekly sets per muscle, so you know what you're actually training

YOUR SPLIT, YOUR WAY
• Pick a proven split, look at every day before you switch
• Or build your own: name it, set the days, pick the exercises
• 200+ exercises, swap or superset anything in two taps

WORKS EVERYWHERE
• Syncs between your phone and computer
• Works offline in the gym
• No ads. No tracking. Your data is yours — delete it anytime.
